Connect and Intersect

Connect: every phone in a waiting room to one nurse.

Intersect: the moment a patient gets worse and the moment someone notices.

Today those two moments are hours apart.

Vigil. The waiting room gets a monitor.

Triage is a snapshot.

Two minutes at arrival: stable, wait.

Then hours in a chair. Re-checks while waiting are local policy, hours apart, and audits find them missed.

Early sepsis, slow bleeding, a heart attack in progress: the body compensates, then it doesn't.

Waiting is where people get worse unseen.
