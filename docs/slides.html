Connect & Intersect

Connect: every phone in a waiting room → one nurse.

Intersect: the moment a patient gets worse  ×  the moment someone notices.

Today those two moments are hours apart.

Vigil — the waiting room gets a monitor.

Triage is a snapshot.

Two minutes at arrival: "stable — wait."

Then hours in a chair. Re-checks while waiting are local policy, often hours apart, and audits find them missed.

Early sepsis, slow bleeding, an evolving heart attack: the body compensates, then it doesn't.

Waiting is where people get worse unseen.

What it measures — and what it doesn't

Heart rate · breathing rate · heart-rate variability · perfusion · capillary refill time · room breathing.

Physics, not AI: blood absorbs light; the camera sees your finger pulse 120 times a second — the same signal as the clip in a hospital.

Not oxygen saturation. Not a blood-pressure number. Not a diagnosis.

It doesn't prevent deterioration. It shortens how long it goes unnoticed.
